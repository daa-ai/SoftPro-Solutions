Before you get in touch

A little preparation
goes a long way.

There’s no need to have every detail figured out. A few starting points can make the first conversation more useful.

Helpful things to bring

  • 01Your location
    The suburb, town, or area where the work would happen.
  • 02Your ideas
    A short description, inspiration images, or plans you already have.
  • 03Your priorities
    What matters most, plus any timing or budget range you’re comfortable sharing.

Still at the early-idea stage? That’s a perfectly good place to start.

What should I include in my first enquiry?

Tell us where the project is, what you’re hoping to do, and what stage you’re at. If you have a rough timeline, budget range, photos, or plans, those can help too—but you can get in touch without them.

Can I reach out before I have plans?

Yes. Share your early ideas and questions. We can discuss what information may be useful next and whether the project fits the services Altrion offers.

How do costs and timing get worked out?

They depend on the project scope, site, selected materials, and other requirements. Share what you know, and ask for the scope, assumptions, and next steps to be clarified before making decisions.

What if I’m not sure which service I need?

Describe the outcome you want—such as a new home, more room, roof work, or help with plans. The first conversation can help clarify the right next question.
